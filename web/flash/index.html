ColorMesh installer

Connect the board with a USB cable, pick a channel and press Install. Works in Chrome or Edge on a computer. One board is enough: the others take the new firmware from it over Bluetooth.

How a board works

Hold the screen
every board nearby shows your colour; several holders mix.
Tap the screen
a wave of your colour and its opposite on every board: the centre makes rings, an edge makes bands moving in from that side. Waves from several boards merge.
Swipe right
the colour ring: tap a colour to change yours.
Swipe left
the system page: version, Bluetooth address, the boards nearby.
Dev mode (on)
a board that hears a newer firmware nearby pulls it over Bluetooth and restarts on it.
This browser can't talk to USB devices: use Chrome or Edge on a computer. USB access needs a secure page (https or localhost).

No board at hand? Try it in your browser.

Configure over USB

With the board plugged in (and the Install dialog closed: it holds the port), connect to read and change its colour, name, dev mode and brightness. Nothing leaves your computer.
